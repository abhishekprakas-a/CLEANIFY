## 0. About Cleanify (what the app tells us)

Cleanify is a professional **cleaning-services provider** with a tech-enabled
booking and operations platform. Customers book online; **certified, background-
checked technicians** carry out the work; every job is documented with **before &
after photos** that the customer approves from their phone. Behind the scenes the
platform runs scheduling, a field-technician mobile app (PWA), photo approvals,
customer reviews, dashboards and reports.

**Services offered [From app]:** Water tank cleaning · Washroom cleaning ·
Interlock cleaning · Solar panel cleaning · Pressure washing · Car wash.

**Who it serves [From app]:** homes, apartments/housing societies, offices,
hospitals, and commercial/industrial sites.

**Positioning [From app]:** *"Clean water tanks, guaranteed spotless"* — reliable,
documented, eco-friendly cleaning you can book in minutes.
